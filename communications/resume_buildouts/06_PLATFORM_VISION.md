# The Platform Vision — TradeG8 as a Hub (Mandy, 2026-06-12)

This is the standing vision, in Mandy's framing. Every feature decision should
trace back to one of these pillars. The enemy is the gap between
pre-apprenticeship and apprenticeship — and the middlemen who exploit the
people stuck in it.

## The hub

One space where students, navigators/case managers, job seekers, contractors,
and mentors all log in. Everyone has a profile. Nobody retypes their life for
every application, and nobody has to cold-call strangers to find an opening or
a mentor.

## Pillar 1 — Students & job seekers (BUILT, June 2026)

- Profile once (apply-once data; works for people with no device via
  navigator-assisted accounts).
- The scrubber finds real, vetted jobs (L&I verified, scored 0–100; fake and
  data-harvesting postings never reach the student).
- One-tap apply; everything auto-logged (tracker + compliance evidence).

## Pillar 2 — Contractors (the flip)

Instead of students chasing contractors, contractors pitch students:
- Contractor profile pages — "learn about us," LinkedIn-style: who we are,
  what trades, what we pay, whether hours count, why you should pick US.
- Contractors list their own jobs directly (still L&I-verified and scored —
  being on the platform doesn't exempt anyone from vetting).
- Contractors search/browse applicants who opted in.
- The premise: contractors are always looking for great people; the platform
  makes the good ones visible and verifiable.

## Pillar 3 — Community & mentorship (Reddit by trade)

Nobody in construction has time to mentor one-on-one — so mentorship becomes
a community structure instead:
- One community per trade (electricians join the electrician side, etc.).
- Channels inside each trade community (like subreddits): General,
  Mentorship, Applications & Ranking, Hold-Over Jobs.
- Mentors are flagged members inside their trade's community — answering
  where everyone benefits, not in private one-on-ones that don't scale.
- The Applications & Ranking channel carries the "what you should be doing
  while you wait / how to jump rank" content (the grilling curriculum's
  rank-up cheat sheets become living community content).

## Pillar 4 — Compliance & navigators (partially built)

- Case managers see their students, applications, and activity.
- Activity logs become BFET/unemployment/DOC reports.

## The why

"There's a gap between pre-apprentices and apprenticeship... and the people
in the middle always take advantage of those looking for this bridge."
The platform IS the bridge, owned by the community it serves: free for
students, verifiable for contractors, structured so mentorship scales.

## Anti-goals (carry over from standing decisions)

Never: charge students, sell data, premium tiers, ads, gated features.
Every employer on the platform gets L&I-verified — no pay-to-skip-vetting.
